How it works

From your kitchen to every table, in days.

01

We scan your dishes

We visit your kitchen and capture each signature plate in 3D, in about ten minutes per dish — real food, real plating, no illustrations.

02

Your menu goes live

Your name, colours and dishes, with prices, descriptions, pairings and allergens. A QR code for every table, ready to print.

03

Guests see it on the table

One tap places the dish at true size on the table through the phone camera. iPhone and Android, straight from the browser.

For restaurants

A menu that sells the plate, not just the words.

Confident ordering

Guests see the portion, the plating and the size before they choose — especially helpful for visitors, first-timers and guests who don't speak the language.

A memorable first impression

Seeing a dish appear on the table is a moment guests talk about and share — the kind of detail that fits a premium dining room.

Fewer questions at the table

“What does it look like?” and “Is it big?” are answered on the phone, so your team can focus on hospitality.

You stay in control

Change dishes, prices, photos and allergens yourself from a simple admin page. Updates go live in about a minute — no reprinting.
